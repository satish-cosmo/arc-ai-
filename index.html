The Gap

You can finish a tutorial. Can you defend a design decision?

Bootcamps and courses teach you to follow instructions. They don't teach you to make the calls: when to shard a database, how to handle a partial failure, what happens when a queue backs up. That's the part interviews test — and the part nobody grades you on until the job depends on it.
